What Rushcut does

Stop spending evenings scrubbing through footage.

Rushcut watches all of your footage, picks the best scenes and lays them on the beat – originals stay untouched, and the result is a timeline you keep editing.

Read clips

One folder including all subfolders – GoPro, iPhone, Android. Originals are never changed.

Rate scenes

Motion, sharpness, exposure, shake and audio – every scene gets a score.

Detect speech

“Narrated” style: whoever talks to the camera stays in until the last word – the music is ducked underneath.

Use GoPro sensor data

Riding intensity, GPS speed, HiLight tags, face detection (selfies are left out); upside-down cameras are rotated.

Cut to the beat

Chapters per recording day, cut length depending on style and motion. 30 royalty-free songs built in, or your own music.

Themes for DaVinci Resolve

How it works

Six steps to a film

One window, six decisions. When the film is ready you hear a short ping.

  1. 1

    Clips

    Choose a folder or drop it onto the window – subfolders are included.

  2. 2

    Style

    Dynamic, Travel, Calm, Epic or Narrated – sets cut length, shot selection and music.

  3. 3

    Theme

    None or one of five looks for DaVinci Resolve – previewed with your own footage.

  4. 4

    Length

    30 seconds to 30 minutes. With music, the film ends with the song.

  5. 5

    Music

    None, matching the style or your own song. Listen with ▶, “Other song” picks the next one.

  6. 6

    Output

    Choose a target folder – everything for further editing in one place.

~3 minfirst analysis for roughly 20 GB of footage
~1 sfor every new edit with a different style, theme, length or song
0 uploadseverything runs on your computer

The result

A timeline, not a finished video

The output folder contains the timeline as XML and EDL, the songs used, a report, import instructions and a small preview video you can play right in the app. Clips are linked – you keep editing with the originals.

  • V1video
  • A1original audio, lowered and linked to V1 – speech at full level in the “Narrated” style
  • A2music, ducked automatically under speech

Themes for DaVinci Resolve

A look in one click – and everything stays editable

With a theme, Rushcut puts a script into Resolve’s menu. Open a project, choose Workspace → Scripts → Rushcut – <Film>: the script builds a new timeline with color look, transitions, motion, bars, overlays and an opening title. Every effect sits in the timeline on its own.

Works in the free version of Resolve too (tested with Resolve 21). Filmora and EDIUS get the cut without the theme.

MapClip · travel diary

Holiday photos become a film with a map

Film | MapClip at the top of the app switches modes. A folder of photos (JPG, HEIC, PNG) becomes a slideshow with slow zooms, soft dissolves and, if you like, music on the beat. You pick the photos in a grid.

  • Street map in the corner: where the photo was taken, with pin, route and place names
  • Map flight full-screen at every change of place
  • Title and dates suggested from the photos – country, regions, travel dates
  • Video and timeline: a finished 1920×1080 MP4 to share, plus an XML to keep editing

About 40 photos are done in roughly a minute. Place and date come from the photos themselves (GPS, capture time).

FAQ

Good to know

Are my videos or photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Rushcut runs entirely on your computer – no uploads, no account, no tracking. Originals are only read, never changed. The one connection to the internet: for the street map in MapClip, Rushcut loads map sections of the places you visited from OpenFreeMap. You can turn that off in the settings – you then get a simple map from bundled data.

How is Rushcut different from GoPro Quik?

Quik delivers a finished video and needs its app and cloud. Rushcut delivers a suggested edit as a timeline you keep editing freely in DaVinci Resolve, Filmora or EDIUS – with the original clips in full quality.

Do I need the paid version of DaVinci Resolve for the themes?

No. The theme script runs in the free version too (tested with Resolve 21). Filmora and EDIUS get the cut without the theme – you add look and transitions there yourself.

Which cameras are supported?

Anything that writes common video files – GoPro, iPhone, Android and others. For GoPro cameras with sensor data (GPMF), Rushcut also uses speed, riding intensity, HiLight tags and orientation.

Can I use the built-in music on YouTube?

Yes. The songs are by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com) under CC BY 4.0 – including monetized videos. Rushcut puts the music credits into the output folder; they belong in the video description or end credits.

How long does an edit take?

The first analysis takes a few minutes (about 3 minutes for roughly 20 GB of footage). After that, a new edit with a different style, theme, length or music is ready in about a second. The preview video takes only a few seconds per minute of film.

Which languages does Rushcut speak?

English and German – following the system language, switchable in the settings. That includes the report, instructions and folder names in the output.
